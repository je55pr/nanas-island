# Nana's Island - Early Design Notes

## Core loop
Play match-3 levels -> earn restoration/plant rewards -> improve the island -> attract new butterflies -> unlock story, outfits, habitats, and minigames.

## Butterfly sanctuary
Butterflies are persistent visitors, not loot. Specific flowers and habitat conditions attract real species such as Monarchs and Cabbage Whites. Once discovered, they visibly fly around the sanctuary and are recorded in a field guide/collection list.

Discovery should feel observational and generous: the player prepares the right environment, then the butterfly chooses to appear. Avoid arbitrary rarity rolls or monetised acquisition.

## Main characters
Two anthropomorphic leads:
- African elephant: composed, stylish, practical, expressive, with a strong visual silhouette and outfit potential.
- Gibbon: energetic, eccentric, physically playful, long-armed, and a source of comedy.

Their chemistry can borrow the broad contrast the player enjoys in character comedies without copying existing characters.

## Minigames
Short, simple games break up match-3 play. Pong is the first target. Other possibilities include Breakout, memory pairs, simple timing games, and animal-themed arcade diversions. Once discovered through story progression, minigames should remain freely replayable from a permanent island location.

## Player profile
Primary player prefers match-3 games, long-term progression, butterflies, elephants, monkeys, and yellow. Previous favourites include Candy Crush, Pet Saga, 4 Elements, Bejeweled, Monster Busters, Pet Rescue, and Dream Mania. She dislikes common mobile-game dark patterns.
